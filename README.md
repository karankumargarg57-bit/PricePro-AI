# 🚀 PricePro AI — Advanced GST Price Calculator

An advanced, feature-rich Indian GST price calculator web application built with pure **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript**. Designed for seamless calculations, instant product lookup, and professional invoice generation directly in the browser—with zero external dependencies.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🧠 Smart Product & HSN Search:** Instantly filters through an embedded master dataset of products, categories, and HSN/SAC codes.
- **⚡ Automatic GST Lookup:** Automatically determines the appropriate GST rate and tax rules based on the selected product and pricing tiers.
- **🎛️ Flexible Tax Modes:** 
  - *GST Exclusive* (GST added extra to the base price)
  - *GST Inclusive* (GST extracted backward from the total price)
- **📊 Advanced Item Management:** Handle custom quantities, dynamic line discounts (0%–100%), and manual GST overrides seamlessly.
- **🧾 Comprehensive Summary & Invoice Printing:** View itemized breakdowns, total quantity, taxable value, CGST/SGST/IGST splits, and print or save clean, professional invoices directly (`window.print()`).
- **📋 Quick Actions:** One-click copy results to clipboard and easy session clearing.
- **📱 Fully Responsive UI:** Polished, modern glassmorphism design that looks and works flawlessly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
